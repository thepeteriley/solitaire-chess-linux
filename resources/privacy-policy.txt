Solitaire Chess Privacy Policy
Effective November 2, 2026

Solitaire Chess runs on your device. The app does not collect or send your games,
positions, engine analysis, or personal information to the developer. It has
no account system, advertising, tracking, analytics SDK, or remote backend.

Your game, moves, playing mode, orientation, chosen colour, and computer
strength are saved locally to restore your session. Engine analysis runs
locally using Stockfish on your device (the system package on Linux; bundled on Apple platforms). No internet connection is
required for play or analysis.

You can start a new game to replace the saved game. Changing a preference
replaces the saved value for that preference. On Apple platforms, App Store transactions and any
diagnostics you choose to share with Apple are handled by Apple under Apple's
policies and your system settings.

Support and privacy questions: https://thepeteriley.github.io/solitaire-chess-support/
Full public policy: https://thepeteriley.github.io/solitaire-chess-support/privacy.html

The support site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics scripts,
advertising, embedded trackers, or contact forms added by the developer.
GitHub may process technical visit information to operate its hosting service.
Support uses public GitHub Issues and requires a GitHub account to post.
Do not post private information. The public policy explains these services
and links to GitHub's privacy statement.
